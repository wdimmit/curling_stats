What the site does

It charts the games of DFW Curling Club's events from the club's public YouTube streams: where each rock stopped, its path, its speed and curl, the score, and how long each end took, with a short clip of each rock. Software reads all of this from video, and it is often wrong. Treat every figure as an estimate. The site is free and is provided as it is. It may change or stop at any time.
