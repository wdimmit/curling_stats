Verdict
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Four-player output is unchanged by Tasks 1-6, resting on the uncapped results:
head equals base-b outright (comparison 5, `identical`) for VXU9xwmugRg and
hOKZoeJNTpM; head equals base-a outright (comparison 3, `identical`, never
capped) for AEqLTgM25Tc; and head-nl equals base-nl-a outright (comparison 4,
`identical` x3) for all three videos. Every one of those matches is either an
outright `identical` or, in the base-b case, a fresh two-way comparison run
specifically to avoid resting on two truncated 20-path lists agreeing by
coincidence. Comparison (3)'s capped byte-for-byte match against base-a is
consistent with this but was not, on its own, conclusive for VXU9xwmugRg --
comparison (5) is what makes it so. With the noisiest passes disabled
(--no-longview), HEAD and BASE match exactly and without qualification
(comparison 4).

Caveat: the noise floor did not vanish under --no-longview as the brief
expected (comparison 2) -- the same end-boundary and AEqLTgM25Tc scoring jitter
recurred, meaning the source of nondeterminism is not confined to the longview
pass. This is a pre-existing property of the pipeline on this GPU worker, out
of scope for this task to fix, and worth the controller's attention separately
from the doubles-format-plumbing parity question.
